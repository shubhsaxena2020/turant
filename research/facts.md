# Verified facts: voice-first cyber-fraud reporting (India)

Compiled 2026-11-21. Labels: **[Official]** means a government source, or Parliament figures quoted by reputable media. **[Media]** means news reporting. **[UNVERIFIED]** means I could not confirm it from a primary source. pib.gov.in returned 403 to automated fetches, so Parliament figures are cited through PTI-syndicated coverage and All India Radio (newsonair.gov.in, which is government-run).

## 1. Losses and complaints
- **2024:** citizens lost **₹22,845.73 crore**, up about 206% from **₹7,465.18 crore** in 2023. There were **36,37,288** financial-fraud incidents on NCRP + CFCFRMS (24,42,978 in 2023). Source: MoS Home Bandi Sanjay Kumar, Lok Sabha written reply, 22 Jul 2025 [Official]. https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/business/2025/07/22/del23-lsq-cyber-fraud.html · https://scroll.in/latest/1084796/scroll_in
- **2025:** **₹22,495 crore** reported lost on NCRP, across **24,02,579** financial-fraud complaints. Source: MoS Home, Rajya Sabha unstarred Q.1341, reported 11 Feb 2026 [Official]. https://www.dynamitenews.com/national/cyber-frauds-mount-to-rs22495-crore-in-2025-over-rs8000-crore-saved-through-rapid-response-system
- **Elderly victims, 2025:** **1,03,488** senior citizens filed complaints, with losses of **₹4,005.12 crore**. That is about 4.3% of complaints but about 18% of the amount lost (the share figures are a media calculation). Women filed 4,63,114 complaints, with losses of ₹3,764.51 crore. Source: MHA Rajya Sabha reply, about 5 Aug 2026 [Official]. https://thenewsmill.com/2026/08/over-1-lakh-senior-citizens-and-4-63-lakh-women-report-rs-7769-crore-cyber-fraud-in-2025/ · https://thecsrjournal.in/over-1-lakh-senior-citizens-4-63-lakh-women-reported-financial-cyber-fraud-complaints-2025/
- **Rate of victimisation:** one person falls prey to cybercriminals every 3.5 seconds, or about 97-100 an hour. Source: Amit Shah, 10 Feb 2026 [Official statement, reported by media]. https://theprint.in/india/keep-enough-staff-on-cybercrime-helpline-amit-shah-tells-states-to-improve-response-time-to-1930-calls/2851357

## 2. Money saved through CFCFRMS / 1930, and why speed matters
- **Latest:** more than **₹11,158 crore** saved in more than **32.80 lakh** complaints, as of **30 Jun 2026**. Source: MoS Home, Rajya Sabha written reply, 12 Aug 2026 [Official]. https://www.outlookmoney.com/retirement/cyber-fraud-rs-11158-crore-saved-across-328-million-complaints-how-to-report-financial-fraud-on-cybercrime-portal
- **Earlier milestones:** ₹8,189 crore saved in 23.61 lakh complaints by 31 Dec 2025 (Rajya Sabha, Feb 2026). More than ₹7,000 crore saved in more than 23 lakh complaints (AIR, 17 Dec 2025) [Official]. https://www.newsonair.gov.in/over-rs-7000-crore-saved-through-citizen-financial-cyber-fraud-reporting-and-management-system
- **Stated purpose:** CFCFRMS was launched in 2021 "for immediate reporting of financial frauds and to stop siphoning off funds by the fraudsters" [Official]. Same AIR link.
- **Context for the saved figure:** ₹11,158 crore is roughly a quarter of the ₹45,000+ crore reported lost in 2024-25 combined. This is my own arithmetic. "Saved" means blocked or put on hold. It does not mean money refunded to victims.
- **Mule accounts:** the I4C Suspect Registry has shared **27.37 lakh Layer-1 mule accounts** with banks, helping prevent more than ₹9,518 crore in transactions. Data as of 31 Jan 2026, from a Lok Sabha reply [Official, via media]. https://www.newkerala.com/news/a/fraudsters-increasingly-using-india-based-mule-accounts-move-stolen-469.htm
- **I4C–RBIH agreement:** an MoU signed on 12 May 2026 feeds Suspect Registry and NCRP data into RBIH's MuleHunter.AI. https://www.policyedge.in/p/i4c-and-rbih-partner-to-use-ai-for-detecting-mule-accounts-and-financial-fraud
- **Golden hour:** Hyderabad Cyber Crime DCP said "every minute matters". Victims who report within about 1-3 hours may recover funds. Holds follow the money from bank to bank, layer by layer (Jul 2024) [Police, via media]. https://telanganatoday.com/golden-hour-rule-to-rescue-of-cyber-fraud-victims-in-hyderabad
- **[UNVERIFIED]** A "70-80% recovery if reported within 2 hours vs 15-20% later" figure circulates in media. I found no official source for it. I also found no official figure for how many minutes or hours funds take to move across mule layers. Don't quote either as fact.

## 3. NCRP complaint form, the 1930 helpline, and where it falls short
- **Portal checklist:** read directly from the live cybercrime.gov.in JavaScript bundle on 2026-11-21 [Official, primary].
  - **Mandatory:** incident date and time.
  - **Mandatory:** incident details of at least **200 characters**, with no special characters (# $ @ ^ * ~ | !).
  - **Mandatory:** a copy of the complainant's national ID (Voter ID, DL, Passport, PAN or Aadhaar), as JPG or PNG up to 5 MB.
  - **Mandatory for financial fraud:** **name of bank/wallet/merchant, Transaction ID/UTR no., date of transaction, fraud amount**.
  - **Mandatory:** evidence files, up to 10 MB each.
  - **Optional:** suspect website URL or social handle, and suspect mobile, email, bank account no., address, photo or other ID document.
  - **Suspect identifier types in the form:** UPI ID, Bank Account Number, Mobile, E-mail, Website URL, Social Media, Mobile App.
  - Source: https://cybercrime.gov.in/
- **Login and steps:** select your state, enter mobile and email, get an OTP, solve a captcha, then fill in incident, suspect and complainant details. Preview, confirm, and receive a Complaint ID by SMS and email [Media]. https://www.outlookmoney.com/retirement/cyber-fraud-rs-11158-crore-saved-across-328-million-complaints-how-to-report-financial-fraud-on-cybercrime-portal. The OTP, mobile and captcha fields are also present in the portal code.
- **Languages:** the current portal code has a language selector with **English plus 22 languages** (Assamese, Bengali, Bodo, Dogri, Konkani, Gujarati, Hindi, Kannada, Kashmiri, Maithili, Malayalam, Manipuri, Marathi, Nepali, Odia, Punjabi, Sanskrit, Santali, Sindhi, Tamil, Telugu, Urdu) [Official, primary]. **[UNVERIFIED]** I could not confirm whether the complaint form itself, and the free-text answers, are actually translated. All of the form text I found in the code is English. No speech input exists.
- **1930 is slow:** in Bihar, each 1930 complaint takes about **16-18 minutes** to register by hand. The state plans AI voice-to-text and an IVR to cut this to 5-6 minutes. Regional-language support is "planned", with no date. Lines are proposed to double from 25 to 50 (25 May 2026) [State officials, via media]. https://patnapress.com/?p=75992
- **Unanswered calls:** Amit Shah asked states to "deploy enough responders to 1930 call centres". He warned that if calls go unanswered after several rings, victims lose money (10 Feb 2026). ThePrint link above.
- **National revamp:** on 17 Jun 2026 Shah directed that the 1930 helpline be **revamped using AI and multiple languages** [Official]. https://newsonair.gov.in/home-minister-amit-shah-reviews-functioning-of-national-cybercrime-helpline-1930/
- **Karnataka:** the state 1930 line offers an IVR in Kannada, Hindi and English, plus an SMS-linked web bot for callers who can't get through (Apr 2025) [Media]. https://www.deccanherald.com/india/karnataka/1930-helpline-launched-for-cyber-crime-related-complaints-in-karnataka-3504970

## 4. Scale of UPI, and RBI fraud data
- **UPI, Oct 2026:** a record **25.47 billion** transactions worth **₹31.53 lakh crore**, up 23% in volume and 16% in value year on year. That averages about 822 million transactions and about ₹1.01 lakh crore a day [NPCI data, via media, 2 Nov 2026]. https://www.freepressjournal.in/business/upi-transactions-hit-2547-billion-in-october-value-soars-to-3153-lakh-crore
- **RBI Annual Report 2025-26:** card, internet and digital-payment frauds reported by banks were 28,836 cases / ₹1,452 cr in FY24, 13,332 / ₹517 cr in FY25, and **293 / ₹29 cr in FY26**. Total bank frauds in FY26 were 10,114 cases / ₹48,021 cr. The RBI is exploring a "kill switch" and a possible 1-hour delay on digital payments above ₹10,000 [RBI, via media, 31 May 2026]. https://www.outlookmoney.com/banking/fraud-cases-fall-but-amount-involved-climbs-to-rs-48021-crore-in-fy26-says-rbi-annual-report
  - **Caveats:** the RBI counts cases by the date they were reported to it. The FY26 drop is unexplained in the coverage. The RBI's tables probably cover only frauds of ₹1 lakh and above **[UNVERIFIED]**. RBI data therefore badly undercounts what citizens report to NCRP. Use the I4C figures as your headline numbers.

## 5. Indian languages, voice, rural users
- **IAMAI-Kantar 2024** (released Jan 2025): **886 million** active internet users, 488 million rural and 397 million urban. **870 million (98%)** used the internet in Indic languages. **57%** of urban users prefer Indic-language content. **140 million (15%, "1 in 5")** use voice commands, and **55% of voice users are rural** [Industry body, primary PDF]. https://www.iamai.in/sites/default/files/research/Kantar_%20IAMAI%20report_2024_.pdf
- **IAMAI 2025** (released Jan 2026): about **958 million** users, and 44% use AI features such as voice search [Media]. https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/indias-internet-user-base-tops-950-million-as-rural-growth-ai-drive-adoption-513353-2026-01-29

## 6. Voice tooling: Bhashini and others
- **BHASHINI** (MeitY's Digital India Bhashini Division) supports **36 text and 23 voice languages**, plus 35+ international languages. It has 350+ models, 15M+ inferences a day, and powers 500+ government websites. Services include ASR, TTS, MT, language detection, speaker diarization and keyword spotting (12 Mar 2026) [Official]. https://www.newsonair.gov.in/bhasha-interface-for-india-bhashini-enables-availability-of-advanced-ai-models/
  - **Caveat:** a Feb 2026 PIB backgrounder says **22** voice languages. Late-2024 government material said 13. Check the per-language ASR/TTS availability on bhashini.gov.in before you claim coverage for a specific language. https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2026/feb/doc202629784101.pdf
- **VoicERA:** an open-source, end-to-end voice-AI stack on BHASHINI infrastructure, launched at the India AI Impact Summit in Feb 2026 with EkStep, IIIT-B and AI4Bharat. Its language list and licence are not stated. https://negd.gov.in/press_release/voicera-launched-on-bhashini-national-infrastructure-at-india-ai-impact-summit-2026/
- **[UNVERIFIED]** I found no evidence that NCRP or the national 1930 helpline uses Bhashini today. I also found no live national system that turns a spoken, multilingual description into an NCRP complaint. The closest efforts are the Bihar IVR and voice-to-text pilot and the June 2026 order for a national AI and multilingual revamp. That is the gap this product fills.
